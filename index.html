POWER BI PORTFOLIO PROJECT

Automotive Manufacturing Operations Dashboard

Simulated Manufacturing Dataset

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard for an automotive brake components manufacturing environment, integrating production, quality, inventory, sales, workforce, delivery, and supplier performance data.

  • Analyzed approximately 320K units of production and compared actual output against production targets.
  • Monitored production efficiency, machine downtime, rejection quantity, and a 2.69% rejection rate.
  • Analyzed sales performance across 900 orders, approximately 218K units sold, and 7.36M in simulated sales revenue.
  • Evaluated inventory levels, material consumption, procurement costs, supplier performance, and delivery performance.
  • Built interactive KPI cards, charts, slicers, and dashboard pages to support manufacturing and supply chain performance analysis.

Tools & Skills: Power BI • Power Query • DAX • Microsoft Excel • Data Modeling • KPI Analysis • Dashboard Development

LEAN SIX SIGMA • DMAIC

Assembly Line Efficiency Improvement

Qtech Group AB | Anderstorp, Sweden | Sep–Dec 2025

Applied the Six Sigma DMAIC methodology to analyze the Nova C Double Back assembly process, identify non-value-added activities, investigate root causes of delays, and develop practical improvement proposals.

  • Conducted time studies across 8 modules and 10 assembly steps, covering 80 measured process steps.
  • Developed a Value Stream Map identifying 71.3% value-added time and 28.7% non-value-added/waiting time.
  • Used Pareto Analysis, Fishbone Analysis and 5-Why Analysis to identify major causes of assembly delays.
  • Developed improvement proposals using Point-of-Use Storage (POUS), material segregation, workplace organization and 5S.
  • Established control recommendations to support standardized work and sustain process improvements.

Methods & Tools: DMAIC • Value Stream Mapping • SIPOC • Pareto Analysis • Fishbone Diagram • 5 Whys • 5S • POUS • Time Study • Root Cause Analysis

SOCIO-TECHNICAL SYSTEMS • AI & WMS

Gripen Wheels AB | Sweden | Jan–May 2025
