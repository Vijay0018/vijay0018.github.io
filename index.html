LEAN SIX SIGMA • DMAIC

Assembly Line Efficiency Improvement

Qtech Group AB | Anderstorp, Sweden | Sep–Dec 2025

Applied the Six Sigma DMAIC methodology to analyze the Nova C Double Back assembly process, identify non-value-added activities, investigate root causes of delays, and develop practical improvement proposals.

  • Conducted time studies across 8 modules and 10 assembly steps, covering 80 measured process steps.
  • Developed a Value Stream Map identifying 71.3% value-added time and 28.7% non-value-added/waiting time.
  • Used Pareto Analysis, Fishbone Analysis and 5-Why Analysis to identify major causes of assembly delays.
  • Developed improvement proposals using Point-of-Use Storage (POUS), material segregation, workplace organization and 5S.
  • Established control recommendations to support standardized work and sustain process improvements.

Methods & Tools: DMAIC • Value Stream Mapping • SIPOC • Pareto Analysis • Fishbone Diagram • 5 Whys • 5S • POUS • Time Study • Root Cause Analysis

Gripen Wheels AB | Sweden | Jan–May 2025

Analyzed warehouse operations and developed a socio-technical transformation approach for integrating AI-enabled solutions with Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), considering technology, people, processes, and organizational requirements.
