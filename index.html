PROJECT 01 · LEAN SIX SIGMA · DMAICQTECH GROUP AB · SEP–DEC 2025

Assembly Line Efficiency Improvement

Team project (3 members) · Anderstorp, Sweden

DEFINE→MEASURE→ANALYZE→IMPROVE→CONTROL
  • Applied the DMAIC methodology to a product assembly line, timing 8 modules across 10 steps (80 timed steps) with three observers who agreed the timing rules first.
  • Value Stream Mapping showed 38 min 55 s on average per module, with 28.7% of it waiting (non-value-added) time.
  • Pareto, Fishbone (6M) and 5-Why traced the biggest delays to searching for parts (19.4% of waiting time, 30.9% together with setup), caused by mixed pallets and a layout built for storage, not for the work.
  • Compared five solutions with an Impact–Effort matrix and a weighted decision matrix; Point-of-Use Storage (POUS) ranked highest, backed by 5S, a new layout and a control plan.

AI & WMS Roadmap

Team project (5 members) · Jönköping, Sweden

  • Analysed a tire-and-wheel warehouse of about 51,000 m² with the Davis et al. socio-technical framework, using site visits, interviews and process mapping.
  • Identified floating-bin storage with no fixed locations, paper-based tracking, Jeeves ERP and LAPTU not fully connected, and a culture that resisted automation.
  • Recommended a 3-phase roadmap: build trust through workshops, set up data foundations (barcodes, cycle count, a data champion), then add an AI-enabled WMS with smart slotting and IoT.

Recommendation: a 3-phase roadmap targeting inventory accuracy up to 99% and up to 20% better space use, with shorter picking and order lead times.
